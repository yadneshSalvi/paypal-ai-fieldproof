# Private lifecycle drafts

`/lifecycle` joins an independently held project profile with a separate organization profile. Donors and volunteers can opt into their own private event inbox. Organization owners and coordinators can explicitly enable bounded private drafting, review an exact schedule, and inspect its original occurrences. Organization access supplies no project role. Profile names and possession-based entry do not verify a person, organization, email address or financial account.

## Original scope and deadlines

A private project schedule requires the exact current project-organizer session as well as the organization session. An aggregate schedule pins the selected current project disclosures and allowed sections; future affiliations cannot widen it. Organization-only lists retain private-project metadata without its title, definition, source values or body. Another organization member cannot inherit an earlier member's private history.

The reviewed authorization pins organization and project membership versions, roles, original sessions, opt-in version, exact disclosure versions and the earliest original expiry. Pause, replacement, review, reload, return and retry cannot renew it. Disabling and later enabling the organization program creates a new version and does not revive an older production grant. An earlier permitted capture can remain readable until its own original authority or source permission ends.

## Durable occurrences

The UTC cadence is every 15 minutes, hour, day or week, beginning at an explicitly reviewed future time. The stored timezone is a presentation label; due times retain their original UTC identity. Materialization records each original slot before advancing its durable position. A slot paused at its due time stays skipped permanently. A replacement leaves the earlier definition and occurrences intact.

Capture and drafting have separate bounded worker leases and retained attempts. A capture uses one actual repeatable-read transaction cutoff at or after the original due time. Its encrypted source, dependency pins and hash commit before drafting starts. A restarted worker reuses that exact capture. Later project activity can make an earlier edition historical; it cannot replace the saved wording or cutoff. Ready, skipped, withdrawn-authority, expired and cancelled slots cannot become a second production slot.

Source reads acquire the source-control table lock before the first repeatable snapshot. This covers a first withdrawal that inserts a previously absent control row. Current authority and original expiry are checked again after waits. Private bodies and source details disappear when their original grant or source permission ends; encrypted original records remain retained.

The current worker uses authored English and Spanish factual templates with exact scalar citations. Unknown facts remain unknown. Amounts distinguish original gross, credited support, completed returns, current assignments, spending and reserves. Private review authorizes no delivery, model request, payment or physical acceptance. Email wording is an unsent preview with no destination. Spanish has no fluent-human review claim.

## Bounded execution and recovery

`GET /api/cron/lifecycle` requires the operator's existing scheduler bearer secret before any work, rejects URL overrides, and honors the execution hold. One invocation selects at most 5 definitions, 20 due slots per selected definition, 5 pending occurrences, and 5 personal grants. The personal observer persists its encrypted continuation only after the original page commits, including continuation within a long recipient history. An abandoned observer repeats an idempotent page rather than omitting older grants.

Concurrent workers share one occurrence and lease. Repeated exact command intents recover their original minimal receipt; changed payloads conflict. Only a known server-rolled-back local transaction can retry automatically, once. An uncertain external response is never resubmitted by these services. The current worker makes zero provider/model/email calls.
